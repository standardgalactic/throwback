🌀 What Is RSVP?

Most physics starts by asking "what moves?" RSVP starts by asking a different question first: what's even allowed to persist? RSVP treats reality as a continuous field of distinctions — places where the world could go one of several ways — rather than a fixed stage with particles bouncing around on it.

🔧 The Core Move

Instead of writing down forces and solving for trajectories, RSVP asks which configurations are admissible (consistent, repairable, able to continue) and treats everything else as excluded before it ever gets a chance to happen. Persistence isn't something added on top of physics — it's the filter physics has to pass through in the first place.

🪐 Why Bother?

Because "does this survive contact with reality?" turns out to be a surprisingly load-bearing question, whether you're asking it about a particle's worldline, a galaxy's spin, a chain of reasoning, or a civilization's supply lines. RSVP is the attempt to make that one question mathematically precise enough to reuse everywhere.
