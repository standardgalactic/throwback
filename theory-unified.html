🧠 The Big Idea

Every piece on this site — RSVP, Spherepop, Holonomy, all of it — is a different-scale answer to the same underlying question: local coherence is easy to get; global compatibility is not. You can make any small patch of a system look consistent almost trivially. Getting many patches to agree with each other, without quietly papering over a real disagreement, is the actual hard problem — and it shows up again and again wearing different disguises.

🎭 Same Obstruction, Different Costumes

In physics: a gluing defect between two locally-fine patches of geometry. In economics: a ledger where every transaction is locally valid but the system-wide incentive structure is degenerate. In ethics: a set of individually reasonable claims on scarce necessities that can't all be honored at once. Same obstruction, three costumes.

🗺️ How It's Organized

Five parts trace this from the ground up: epistemic foundations (how would you even notice this problem?), the geometry of persistence (this is where Holonomy and Spherepop live), systems architecture (what does an honest system do when it hits the obstruction?), representation and compression (can you compress something without quietly discarding the part that mattered?), and political economy (what happens when the obstruction shows up in how people are allowed to live?).
